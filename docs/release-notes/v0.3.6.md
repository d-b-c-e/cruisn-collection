## Cruis'n Collection v0.3.6 — force feedback built in, plugin retired

- **The emulator now drives your wheel itself.** The FFB Arcade Plugin is
  gone: no `dinput8.dll` / `MAME64.dll` / `FFBPlugin.ini`, no "Detect
  wheel", no wheel GUID, no reset tool. The value each game wrote to its
  arcade wheel motor goes straight to the wheel through SDL2 haptics as
  one signed constant force on the steering axis (the same model as
  Cannonball DX and Flycast), interpreted exactly as the plugin's Cruis'n
  handler did. Forces go to the device you bound as steering in CONTROLS
  SETUP. Thanks to Endprodukt, the plugin's maintainer, for the advice.
- What this fixes on the way: force feedback vanishing after the first
  game of a session, the first-launch device-scan hang, the crash on
  exit, forces left standing on the wheel after quitting, and Exotica
  needing to pretend to be Cruis'n USA. Forces are released half a second
  after a game stops writing its motor (pause, menus), the instant the
  Esc menu opens, and at exit.
- **Feel on a direct-drive base.** The games' force is a kick against
  every wheel movement that keeps pushing for about 150 ms after the
  wheel stops. The arcade wheel's friction absorbed that; a Moza or
  Fanatec base doesn't, and the result was alternating pulls while driving
  straight. New defaults: **FFB STRENGTH 50** for fresh installs and a
  **50 ms low-pass** on the force (`ffb_smooth`), which fixed it on the
  project rig. Two more knobs for stubborn bases, `ffb_damper` and
  `ffb_friction` (INSTALL.md, *Force feedback on a strong wheel*).
- **New SETTINGS → FFB DIRECTION.** Wheel bases disagree on which way a
  positive force turns. The default was measured on a Moza. If your
  wheel runs *away* from centre in Exotica, or shakes at any strength in
  USA, flip it.
- **Pedals on a separate device could bind in CONTROLS SETUP and do
  nothing in game.** The wizard numbers axes the way it sees them; MAME
  names them by fixed DirectInput slot and skips slots a device lacks,
  so a pedal set exposing, say, Y and RZ was written as X and Y. The
  launcher now reads each device's real slot layout and translates.
- **SETTINGS → FFB DIAGNOSTICS** and **SETTINGS → SAVE SUPPORT BUNDLE**
  in the launcher itself. The bundle carries the force trace, a report
  with a plot of force against wheel position, the emulator's own FFB log
  (which device it took, every level sent), and the device axis layouts.

## Known issues

- Force direction and feel on Fanatec bases are untested with the new
  path: FFB DIRECTION and FFB STRENGTH are the knobs; a support bundle
  after a minute of USA tells us the rest.
- **Cruis'n Exotica: manual transmission cannot be selected** (upstream
  emulation gap; the game drives as an automatic).
- Exotica's Amazon track shows glitches in our GL overlay; `exotica_gl =
  0` under `[collection]` in `rig\collection.ini` uses MAME's renderer.
- Faint red/blue specks on the road far ahead in the V-Unit games.
- Pressing LEFT immediately after the launcher opens can start a game as
  if ENTER had been pressed.
