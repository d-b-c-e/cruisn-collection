## Cruis'n Collection v0.3.7 — Exotica feel: crashes shake again, per-game strength

Follow-up to v0.3.6 (force feedback built into the emulator) from the
first evening on the rig.

- **Cruis'n Exotica felt like a weak centering spring and nothing else.**
  Its crash and jump effects turn out to be single-frame full-force spikes
  (17 ms) - the arcade wheel's inertia made a thump of those, a
  direct-drive base makes a tick, and v0.3.6's smoothing shrank it more.
  Two things bring them back: the **rumble burst per force update** the
  old plugin had (`ffb_rumble`, on by default; set it to `0` under
  `[collection]` in `rig\collection.ini` if you prefer a silent wheel),
  and full-size force jumps now pass the smoothing unfiltered.
- **FFB STRENGTH per game.** Each game card (press Enter on a game) has an
  FFB STRENGTH row; blank uses the SETTINGS value. Exotica's spring is
  much softer than the V-Unit kicks, so 100 there with 50 globally is the
  pairing the rig runs.

Everything from v0.3.6 still applies: FFB DIRECTION if a wheel runs away
from centre, `ffb_smooth` (default 50) against the left-right pulls on a
direct-drive base, `ffb_damper` / `ffb_friction` for stubborn bases, and
the pedal axis-slot fix for pedals on their own USB device.

Update from inside the app: SETTINGS → CHECK FOR UPDATES, or
CruisnSetup.exe → Updates...
