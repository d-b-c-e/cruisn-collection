# Off-Road Pike's Peak: later snowy left-turn screen

Pike's Peak is the saved second Off-Road course, but its ten sparse
resident/control views were exact despite extra submitted geometry. The
[earlier fast-turn screen](2026-09-28-offroad-pikes-sharp-turn-screen.md)
found no clear El Paso-like ground opening at 5100..5280. I used the saved
wheel trace and native snapshots to select a different fast left turn in
snowy terrain, then captured the widened completed output before deciding
whether a resident-ground replay had a measurable target.

One frozen `03123d5b272` continuous-3× **control-only** replay passes 6,540
recorded inputs/native comparison, literal FFB0, stable physical 2560×1440
display watch and owned shutdown. It prepares 2,514 host scenes and submits
5,361,186 quads across the prefix. Nineteen completed CRT views at 6340..6520
every ten frames were inspected as a contact sheet. Snow, cliff and road
shoulder remain visible along the outer turn; the inspected sequence shows
no clear lower-margin blue/black opening like the source-qualified El Paso
case. Dark rock, HUD and CRT border pixels are not grounds for labeling a
geometry gap from color counts alone.

This is a negative visual screen for **one** additional Pike's Peak interval,
not a proof that the whole course is free of missing geometry. No resident
candidate replay was justified by these views; the earlier sparse exact
resident pair remains the only second-course candidate evidence. A precise
owner timestamp or a source-qualified positive gap would make another
resident pair useful. Snow/sky similarity can hide a subtle texture defect,
so the inspected sheet does not clear every surface or the user's separate
sharp-turn black-texture report.

The prepare-only plan passed without starting MAME. Local evidence is under
`results/diagnostics/offroad-pikes-peak-20260927-live-1/`:
`left-turn-6340-control-run/report.json` and
`left-turn-6340-control-run/run/gl-snap/pikes-left-turn-contact-v1.png`.
No native code, renderer deployment, personal installation or public
release changed.
