# Native private admissions joined to original command tickets

Private scenery admissions now connect to the original-model observer in MAME.
The diagnostic records a draw only after its future or completed-waiting packet
was successfully queued and its private producer materials were committed.
Actual source bindings, removals and resets maintain the ledger. Each original
CPU command copies its qualification and exact packet/lifetime watermarks into
its ticket; later slot reuse cannot change that queued proof.

The optional `--exotica-endpoint-admit-from` requires the existing explicit
candidate-only endpoint observer and actual private future drawing. It remains
read-only: no endpoint replacement or fade policy is enabled.

## Remaining boundary

The next change is private-target replacement at each qualified original quad's
existing command position. The normal target must receive its original draw;
the private extended target must receive only its replacement. Applying both
to the private target risks double blending. Materials stay at their current
original stream position, and expected versus actual original quads must match
before accepting a replacement. Then validate across-frame fade behavior and
sustained speed. This observer does not itself reduce pop-in or fix black margins.
